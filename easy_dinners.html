Easy Dinner Recipes

Easy Fish Tacos

A flavorful plant-based twist on the classic Mexican dish? Yes, please! These tacos deliver a perfect balance of sweet, smoky, and savory flavors in every bite, making them an irresistible and satisfying option for vegans and taco lovers alike.

Miso Honey Salmon

Pan-seared salmon gets a flavor-boost from a tasty marinade of honey, soy sauce, garlic, and ginger. For even more flavor, the marinade is reduced to a sauce for drizzling over the cooked fish. Pair with steamed broccoli and white rice for a balanced meal.

Chicken Fajitas in Parchment

Wrap up a chicken breast and your favorite fajita veggies and seasonings in a parchment packet for fool-proof cooking and easy clean-up. This recipe serves one, but can easily be scaled up to feed a crowd.
